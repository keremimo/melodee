About

What Melodee is, and what runs on the FM-1 after you install it.

  • Multi-engine synthesizer and sequencer firmware
  • Five instruments, four tracks
  • Based on Felucca; not official M-VAVE firmware
  • Where to report a problem

Before you install

What you need, and what to save before replacing the firmware.

  • Chrome or Edge, and a data USB cable plugged in directly
  • Official V15 can be restored from the installer
  • Projects, Prophet programs, and anything a downgrade would lose
  • Field-testing release; no warranty

Installing

The web installer, the two builds, and a terminal install.

  • Standard (dual core) and Single core
  • When to choose Single core, and what it changes
  • Leave the cable connected while writing
  • Installing from a terminal
  • What the FM-1 reports when the install finishes

The panel

The keys, knobs and buttons, and the lights.

  • Keys, KNOB 1–4, MASTER, ALGORITHM, PRESETS
  • FX, SCL, ENV, LFO, EDIT, GLO, HOME, SAVE, ARP, SEQ, PLAY, REC
  • OCT− and OCT+
  • Key and button lights: dark, dim, bright, breathing

Getting around

How pages, lists, sheets and holds work.

  • SELECT turns the pages of the open section
  • KNOB 1 moves across, KNOB 2 moves down
  • OCT+ enters or confirms, OCT− goes back
  • On Stage the octave buttons shift the octave
  • Holds: FX, GLO, SCL, EDIT, SAVE, HOME, SEQ, REC
  • Sheets, pickers and undo

Stage

The HOME screen.

  • The selected track's four knobs
  • Notes, chords and the live waveform
  • A lane per track: sound, pattern, bar, level
  • Header: tempo, REC, octave shift

Tracks

Four tracks, one instrument each, and the mixer.

  • ALGORITHM selects the track
  • Track colour on the screen
  • Mixer: level, pan, reverb send, mute
  • GLO opens the mixer, then the global pages

Sounds

Browsing and starting a sound.

  • PRESETS opens the browser
  • Factory presets, native slots, general user slots
  • Categories, favourites, recent
  • Changing the engine starts from its init sound
  • Knob acceleration, and holding SAVE to go back

Engines

The instruments a track can play.

  • Selectable: PROPHET, FM6, CZ-1, SID, DRUM
  • Retired engines stay silent; their saved values remain
  • How many notes a track can play

Prophet

Sequential Prophet-5 programs.

  • Init and the factory bank
  • Oscillators, sync, mixer, filter, envelopes
  • Poly-Mod, LFO, wheel, unison, glide
  • User slots P001–P128, and SysEx

FM6

Six-operator FM, DX7-compatible.

  • Algorithms and the on-screen chart
  • Operators, pitch envelope, LFO, controllers
  • MODERN, MARK I and OPL
  • Slots F001–F064, and DX7 SysEx

CZ-1

Native Casio CZ-1 tones.

  • Two lines, windows and the six envelopes
  • Factory tones and slots Z001–Z128
  • Name, copy a line, compare
  • Casio SysEx

SID

A Commodore SID chip for every note.

  • 6581 and 8580
  • Waveforms, ring, sync, envelope, filter
  • Factory presets

Drum

Synthesized 808 and 909 kits.

  • Lanes and the General MIDI key map
  • Tuning, decay, tone, attack or snappy, level
  • A recorded hit can keep its own pitch and length
  • The drum grid

Sound designer

Build a sound on the device, from a goal or from the engine's own pages.

  • Open it from the HOME menu, or from a sound page
  • Engine, start (current, init, or a recipe), and which pages
  • Stages: Features, Source, Tone, Env, Motion, Play, FX
  • Audition on the keys, compare with the original, finish and name it

Saving sounds

Store a sound in a user slot and name it.

  • SAVE, then USER, a slot, and the keys to spell the name
  • What a native Prophet, FM6 or CZ-1 slot keeps
  • General slots U01–U64
  • Loading a sound leaves the patterns alone

Projects

Songs saved on the device, the startup project, and the template.

  • Slots, BOOT, and the template for a new project
  • New song: key, tempo, and a role for each track
  • SAVE + REC writes the project back to its slot
  • Autosave after five idle seconds
  • What a project file contains

Sequencer

How sequencing is organised, and the pages under SEQ.

  • NOTES, PATTERN, TEMPO, PATTERNS, SONG, MOTION
  • 64 steps, division, swing and gate
  • PLAY starts and stops; REC arms the selected track

Patterns

Eight patterns on each track.

  • Hold SEQ and press a white key, or use the grid
  • Copy notes and motion; delete with confirmation
  • A change waits until that track's loop ends
  • Hold SAVE to undo

Notes

The editor for entered steps and recorded takes.

  • Step, pitch, length and velocity
  • Move, zoom, slide, chance and delete
  • Erase with EDIT held while recording
  • Undo and redo
  • Drum hits on the same page

Recording

Live loops, capture, and playback quantize.

  • Arm a track; REC + PLAY records at once
  • Timing, velocity and held length are kept
  • Hold REC to capture the last bars
  • TEMPO QNT snaps playback; OFF restores the take
  • The shared pool of timed notes

Motion

Knob moves recorded into the pattern.

  • Arm REC, start playback, then turn a sound knob
  • Armed and stopped: play first
  • Stopping or bypassing motion restores the patch
  • Events shared across the project

Songs

An arrangement of patterns, or a jam turned into one.

  • Section, track, pattern and repeats
  • Pattern changes while playing are logged
  • Hold REC on SONG to keep that jam
  • Playback, the last section, and stop

Scales and chords

Tuning, quantize, transpose and chord keys.

  • Families, favourites and root
  • QNT: OFF, WHITE, SNAP, ALL, MPC
  • Microtonal scales
  • Transpose, and the octave buttons
  • Chord types and voicings

Arpeggiator

The arpeggiator on ARP and ARP 2.

  • Mode, rate, octaves and gate
  • Swing, probability, hold and order
  • The beat light

Envelopes and modulation

The shared envelope, LFOs and modulation slots.

  • ENV and ENV DEST
  • LFO, LFO 2 and LFO DEST
  • Four modulation slots; MIDI controllers as sources
  • Engines that use their own envelopes instead

Effects

Per-track sends, the shared effects, and the slicer.

  • Distortion, chorus, delay and reverb sends
  • Slicer patterns
  • Delay: DIGI, TAPE, and ping-pong
  • Reverb: ROOM, SPRING, HALL; chorus; the master limiter

Performance effects

The FX hold layer: effects on the keys, macros on the knobs.

  • Repeat, reverse, filter, tape stop, freeze, harmonizer, flanger, phaser
  • Mutes on the black keys
  • The knobs do not change the saved preset
  • Latch, and a double-press to keep it open

Global

Device settings on GLOBAL and SYSTEM.

  • Concert pitch (A4) and fine tune
  • Clock: internal, USB or TRS
  • MIDI input status, the drum channel, and routing
  • What is kept when the power goes off

Menu

Hold HOME.

  • COLOR, SPEAKER, HOLD, LIGHTS
  • USB AUDIO
  • Click, count-in, note preview, chord entry, FX latch
  • Knob acceleration, screen off, calibration
  • About and Sound designer

MIDI

USB and TRS MIDI in and out.

  • Channels 1–4 play tracks 1–4
  • The drum channel, and every other channel
  • The keys send on the track's channel
  • Pitch bend, sustain, panic and clock
  • Microtonal notes on MIDI out

USB audio

Play the computer through the FM-1, or record the four tracks.

  • Melodee Out and Melodee In
  • One mono channel per track, after level and before pan
  • 16- or 24-bit, 44.1 kHz; no driver
  • Switch either device off from the menu
