# MIDI THRU (the menu): OFF, nothing passes; ON, what comes in goes on out where it did not come from (Bluetooth in and
# the TRS jack's to USB, USB's never back to USB); Song Position only from the input CLK follows (INT here: none); the
# setting survives a restart. Channels 6..8: no track plays them (ROUT CH1-4), so what goes out is the thru's alone.
# (The host builds Melodee without Bluetooth: nothing listens there, so what goes to Bluetooth is not checked here.)
wait 3000
expect midi_thru 0
out
midi TRS 95 3C 64
wait 100
expect_no_out USB 95 3C 64
# HOME held: the menu; KNOB 2 down to MIDI THRU (COLOR FONT HOLD LIGHTS USB VOLUME, then it: the host has no
# BLUETOOTH row, the device has it before MIDI THRU); OCT+ turns it ON
press HOME
wait 800
release HOME
wait 200
spin K2 5
wait 100
shot thru_menu.png
tap OCT+
wait 200
expect midi_thru 1
press HOME
wait 800
release HOME
wait 300
out
midi USB 95 3C 64
wait 100
expect_no_out USB 95 3C 64
out
midi BLE 96 40 50
wait 100

out
midi TRS 97 41 51
wait 100
expect_out USB 97 41 51
out
midi TRS F2 10 00
wait 100

expect clock_source 0
# kept: the menu saved it, the restart reads it again
wait 2000
